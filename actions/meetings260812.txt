◊ Tokyo Fintech Webinar – 29 jul 26, wed
Cutting through the noise in Japanese Finance & FinTech
Japan Finsight implements the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard for AI agents to call structured tools and read structured data. Once connected, your AI agent can query Japan's official corporate disclosure data (EDINET) the same way it queries any other tool.

What you can ask
Questions an MCP-connected agent can answer with Japan Finsight tools:

"Show me the past 5 years of cash flow for Toyota."
"Which Japanese mid-cap companies have an activist holder filing in the last 12 months?"
"Walk me through Tokio Marine's recent buybacks and capital policy."
"What material events has Sony filed in the past 6 months?"
"Who are the top 10 shareholders of [any listed company], with bilingual names?"

Founder Matt Helmer will join us to discuss the evolution of the tool, and his approach to Japan stock market research based on it.

Matty Compost 5:27 PM Quick summary of where the Regen Knowledge Commons Toolkit is heading:
• Core problem: knowledge coordination friction. Valuable knowledge already exists, but it is fragmented across organizations, repositories, forums, media, grant reports, local communities, and individual memory.
• Core theory of change: help people move from confusion → clarity → action → shared learning by making knowledge more discoverable, understandable, connected, reusable, and reviewable.
• Evolving architecture follows a knowledge lifecycle:
Capture → Understand → Relate → Compose → Specify → Implement → Learn → Evolve → Steward → Interoperate
• Major layers:
  Ontology & semantic layer
  Encyclopedia & learning layer
  Resource & source-system layer
  Option & pattern library
  Tracks & deployment layer
  Implementation memory layer
  Stewardship & evolution layer
• Increasing focus on source systems (not just resources): organizations, communities, repositories, podcasts, governance forums, media ecosystems, and other living knowledge environments.
• New areas being incorporated:
  Public Goods Builder Commons
  Media Source Systems & Living Artifacts
  Podcast / conversation knowledge graphs
  Open-source tooling reuse and interoperability
  Implementation memory and retrospectives
• Strong influence from CSIS around structural integrity:
  distinguish claims from evidence
  distinguish visibility from reviewability
  make assumptions, authority, risks, and dependencies more explicit
• Long-term vision: A regenerative knowledge commons that helps ecosystems discover what exists, understand how things relate, reuse what works, learn from implementation, preserve shared memory, and continuously improve collective coordination capacity.
